# Full visual surface library

The authored texture families CurbConcrete and SoftStucco are generated by the OPD project from deterministic mathematical noise and surface profiles. They contain no copied photographs, game artwork or generated-image service output. The generator and textures follow the terms of the OPD project. Generation parameters, channel encoding and file hashes are recorded in Textures/PROVENANCE.json.

Existing brick, asphalt, ground, timber, bark and metal scan maps remain attributed to Poly Haven under CC0 1.0. Their source authors, URLs and checksums are retained in the original manifests. [Poly Haven resource licenses](../../ThirdParty/PolyHaven/LICENSES.md)

Existing project-authored roof, paint, fabric and other maps retain their original provenance. [Project texture provenance](../Textures/PROVENANCE.json)

BINDINGS.json records each active material alias, texture family, image paths and metric mapping. It is generated when the library is installed in the saved Street scene. Materials are copied into this folder before replacement. Previous resources remain available for comparison.
