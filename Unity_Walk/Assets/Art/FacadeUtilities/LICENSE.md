# Vine With Branch 3d Model Free

Model and albedo by [iGauravRajput](https://sketchfab.com/iGauravRajput),
distributed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

[Source model on Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/vine-with-branch-3d-model-free-e4665bcbc81a43cb90bf998242a75b6a).

The asset was converted from the supplied GLB to two simplified FBX meshes with normalized dimensions.
The original albedo was reduced to 2048 × 2048. The scene scales the plant to fit the wall and applies
three autumn tints under HDRP lighting.
